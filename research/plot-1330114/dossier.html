    Gaps

    What is not public, and the two-minute way to get it

    These services check a captcha on the server or answer only from inside the UAE. The research did not try to get past either.

    1. Coordinates, zoning and legal status. Open DLD's Property Status Enquiry, choose the Land Number tab, then area Hor Al Anz East, property type Land, land number 1928, sub-number 0. The Municipality Number tab should also accept 1330114. It returns the plot's location and land photo, height, land-use, parking and setback regulations, zone code, Dubai Municipality violations, property blocks and rental disputes.
    2. Makani number and plot outline. Search 1330114 in the Makani or Dubai Now app, or open makani.ae from a UAE connection.
    3. Affection plan. Request it from Dubai Municipality's planning services, which require an owner or consultant account.
