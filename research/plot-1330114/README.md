**What this means for an investor**

- **Non-freehold.** Every one of the 386 parcels in Hor Al Anz East is non-freehold. DLD's own explanation on the Property Status page is that non-freehold property can be bought by UAE and GCC nationals; freehold purchase is open to all nationalities. A foreign investor would need an eligible ownership structure.
- **Commercial land, large for its area.** The plot is the 30th largest of the community's 386 registered parcels, and the 10th largest of the 75 parcels typed Commercial / Commercial. The median parcel in the community is 1,021.93 m² (a standard 11,000 sq ft villa plot), so this plot is over five times that.
- **No recorded transactions.** No sale, mortgage or grant of a 5,370 m² (57,806 sq ft) land parcel in Hor Al Anz East appears in DLD transaction data from 1997 to 1 October 2026 (two public mirrors totalling 2.6 million records), in DLD's 2026 open-data valuations, or in 2026 sales. The plot appears never to have changed hands through a registered DLD procedure.

## How this was gathered

The research sandbox could reach only GitHub and package registries, so `probe.py` runs on a GitHub-hosted runner through `.github/workflows/dubai-plot-probe.yml` and commits what it gets back into `raw/`. A push to `probe.py`, `steps.txt` or `requests.jsonl` on the research branch starts a run. `steps.txt` names the steps to run (all of them when it is absent), and `requests.jsonl` lists extra one-off requests. Once the workflow is on the default branch, it can also be started by hand with a different `plot_id` and `area_name`.

The plot was found by asking the DLD gateway for every land parcel in area 234 and keeping the row whose `PARCEL_ID` is 1330114.
